Keep an observation that was dropped because the retry outbox could not retain it visible until it is superseded. A drop with an object key stays reported until an event of the same monitor and workspace materializes for that key from an observation admitted after the drop (a retained retry admitted before the drop does not count, even when it completes later); a drop without one stays reported until a later observation span of that monitor materializes an event and drops nothing. Quiet ticks, notify holds, observe failures, events for other keys and daemon restarts no longer clear it. While it is outstanding, `monitor explain` fails at the materialization stage and `doctor` fails the monitor's check, naming the error. The doctor report and its JSON add `unretainedObservationLoss`. A drop whose definition was removed, fails to parse or is duplicated is reported in that id's `orphanedMonitorWork` entry, and doctor fails a `dropped-observation:<id>` check for it (also for a definition that fails validation). The doctor report's orphan groups are now `orphanedMonitorWork` (`OrphanedMonitorWork`): each carries `monitorId`, `monitorKind`, and `materializationRetries` or `unretainedObservationLoss` only when present. A live ephemeral monitor's drop and retained work are reported under that monitor in `ephemeralMonitorWork`; once it is cancelled or reaped they stay visible in `orphanedMonitorWork` as a retired ephemeral monitor, and doctor's checks name it as such instead of asking for a definition repair. The history row for a dropped observation records its position in the span, its `objectKey` and its `title`, as bounded, control-free text, and never its payload. The runtime database gains two nullable `observation_history` columns, a nullable admission column on `monitor_events` and on `materialization_retry_outbox`, and three indexes through the additive schema migration.
