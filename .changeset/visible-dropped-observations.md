Keep an observation that was dropped because the retry outbox could not retain it visible until it is superseded. A drop with an object key stays reported until a later event of the same monitor and workspace materializes for that key; a drop without one stays reported until a later observation span of that monitor materializes an event and drops nothing. Quiet ticks, notify holds, observe failures, events for other keys and daemon restarts no longer clear it. While it is outstanding, `monitor explain` fails at the materialization stage and `doctor` fails the monitor's check, naming the error. The doctor report and its JSON add `unretainedObservationLoss`. A drop whose definition was removed, fails to parse or is duplicated is reported in that id's `orphanedMaterializationRetries` entry, and doctor fails a `dropped-observation:<id>` check for it (also for a definition that fails validation). The history row for a dropped observation records its position in the span, its `objectKey` and its `title`, as bounded, control-free text, and never its payload. The runtime database gains two nullable `observation_history` columns and three indexes through the additive schema migration.
